# Signal / a living career map

Subject: private career research. The memorable element is an interactive 2D field of evidence points assembling into a profile-to-opportunity diagram. Its labels explain the actual workflow; no fabricated counts or simulated search progress.

Palette: porcelain #F5F5FC, ink #252448, iris #6555D8, lavender #E7E3FC, coral #E89A85, slate #77778E. Dark mode uses midnight #18182C and pale ink #EBE9FA. Manrope remains the readable product face; large tightly spaced headlines give hierarchy without adding a display-font dependency.

Layout: left aligned workbench, expressive wide research banner, compact search brief on the left, evidence/results on the right. Motion belongs to the career diagram and user actions, with results kept steady for reading.

```
navigation | headline + interactive evidence field
           | search brief | live research / ranked evidence
```

Review: replaced the previous generic lime dashboard palette. Avoid repeated animated entrances; animate the single canvas and responsive diagram nodes. No orbit lines. Particle activity is decorative, explicitly distinct from source progress. Pause, reduced-motion, offscreen/hidden suspension and keyboard-accessible controls are required.

Engine: requirements with alternatives and negation, BM25 candidate retrieval, bounded feedback learning, evidence coverage, important gaps and actionable shortlist summaries. Model learning is opt-in through explicit job labels, never inferred from browsing or application outcomes.
